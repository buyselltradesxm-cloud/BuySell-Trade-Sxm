The Bush MESBSB23E American Fridge Freezer is a side-by-side unit offering serious storage without splitting fridge and freezer across appliances. It's spacious for large households or those who stock up. The fridge handles fresh food, the freezer stores frozen items. The Total No Frost system prevents ice build-up, and Multi Air Flow keeps temperature even, avoiding warm or cold spots. The freezer offers 6 storage compartments and a fast freeze function for batch-cooking or bulk orders. Rated E for energy efficiency, it comes with a 1-year manufacturer's guarantee.
