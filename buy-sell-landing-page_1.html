How it works

Three steps, and it's on the island's noticeboard.

No account approvals, no waiting. Post today, get a message tonight.

01

Snap & post

Photos, price in EUR or USD, pick your area — Marigot, Philipsburg, Grand Case, wherever you are on the island.

02

Chat, not call

Buyers message you in-app. Your phone number stays yours until you decide to share it.
