Buy Sell Trade SXM is a local marketplace for buyers and independent sellers across Saint-Martin and Sint Maarten. Native SwiftUI Discover and Saved screens provide listing discovery, island-side/area filtering, photos and saved listings. Native Post provides camera/photo selection and on-device drafts. Native Messages provides the inbox and seller conversations. Profile, account and subscription screens use the app's web bridge.

Regarding the November 18 Guideline 2.1(a) report: Profile content requires access to https://buyselltradesxm.com. The domain previously had an availability incident. Public DNS resolution and HTTPS access were rechecked on November 22 and are currently working. This is a possible contributor to the reported failure; we have not confirmed its historical cause.

On November 22, Discover and the signed-out Profile/sign-in screen passed WebKit browser checks at the iPad Air 11-inch screen dimensions in portrait (820x1180) and landscape (1180x820). These are browser emulation results, not a physical iPad or iPadOS 27.0.1 acceptance test. A new TestFlight candidate is being prepared for owner testing; its results will be added before App Review resubmission.

Review steps:
1. Browse Discover without signing in. Filter by island side or area, open listing photos and change FR/EN.
2. Tap Profile. When signed out, an account screen opens. Select Log in and use the demo credentials supplied in App Review Information. No new account is required.
3. After login, open Profile to view account details and listings. Open Post, add a photo/title, then leave and return to check the on-device draft.
4. Open a seller listing and contact the seller; use Messages for the inbox and conversations.
5. Under Car rentals, select a vehicle type and check daily and optional seven-day rates. Vans and Auto body repair are also available in the shared category form.
6. Open Pricing to review monthly Pro subscriptions and Restore Purchases using Apple sandbox. The purchase screen includes localized price, monthly duration, renewal terms, Apple Standard EULA and privacy links.
