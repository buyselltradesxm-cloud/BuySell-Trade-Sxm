Review candidate: version 1.0.6, build 1. Please review this candidate in place of the previously rejected 1.0.2 (3).

Buy Sell Trade SXM is a local marketplace for buyers and independent sellers across Saint-Martin and Sint Maarten. The native SwiftUI Browse and Saved screens provide listing discovery, island-side/area filtering, photos and saved listings. Native Post provides camera/photo selection and on-device drafts. Native Messages provides the inbox and seller conversations. Account and subscription screens use the existing web bridge.

Testing and feedback: on November 16 the owner reported successful testing of the current TestFlight app and no remaining issues. Earlier owner-led TestFlight feedback identified that seller-written titles did not follow FR/EN; French and English display titles were added while preserving the seller original for editing. Feedback also requested Vans, Auto body repair, rental vehicle types and seven-day rental rates; these were implemented in 1.0.6. The shared posting, editing, draft, native routing and bilingual title fixtures passed, including 274 category/form combinations. This is owner-led internal testing; external-beta feedback for this candidate has not yet been recorded.

Review steps:
1. Browse without signing in. Filter Saint-Martin/Sint Maarten or an area, open a listing, view photos and change FR/EN to see localized titles.
2. Sign in using the demo account supplied in App Review Information. Test Post with a photo and title; leave and return to confirm the draft.
3. Under Vehicles select Vans, or under Services select Auto body repair. Under Car Rentals choose a vehicle type and enter a daily rate and optional weekly rate for seven days.
4. Open a seller listing and contact the seller; use Messages to view/send a conversation. Free accounts have five messages per listing; Pro accounts have higher access.
5. Open Pricing to test the monthly Pro subscription and Restore Purchases using Apple sandbox. The purchase screen includes price, monthly duration, renewal terms, Apple Standard EULA and privacy links.

Our November 16 response in the review thread addresses the nine questions under guidelines 4.3 and 4.2.6. A follow-up identifies the current candidate and the actual feedback applied. The app is configured for automatic release after approval.
