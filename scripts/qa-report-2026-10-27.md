# App bug review — 2026-10-27

Changes are local and have not been deployed or submitted to app stores.

## Fixes

Both `app-index.js` and `app-marketplace.js` now:

- Keep existing thumbnails aligned with photos when a seller removes a photo.
- Ignore photo compression and draft restoration results belonging to an older form or account. The latest photo selection wins even if earlier processing finishes later.
- Await draft photo conversion before allowing publication. Failed conversions preserve the current form and permit retry.
- Prevent concurrent submissions from the same form.
- Snapshot upload files so changing photo selection during upload cannot alter validation of the submitted request.
- Stop before writing a listing if the account or form changed during upload. Ignore stale save results after logout or a form change, including delayed detail opening.
- Reject signed-out publication without crashing.
- Cancel pending draft-save timers when initializing a different form.
- Complete FileReader fallback on read errors or aborts instead of leaving photo preparation pending forever.

The initial regression reproduced six failed cases per route before the fixes. The final suite contains 13 assertions per route (26 total), all passing. It is included in `test:qa` and can be run with `npm run test:listing-editor-races` while serving the app on port 5173.

## Follow-up review

Fixed additional asynchronous listing-action and report bugs on both routes:

- Sold, renewal, deletion, and reminder-removal responses no longer alter the displayed listings, favorites, or notices after the account session changes.
- Concurrent owner actions on the same listing are rejected until the pending action finishes. Both successful and failed requests release the lock, allowing retries.
- Repeated submissions from the same report form issue only one request.
- A report response cannot close or reset a newer report form or display a stale success notification after logout.
- Failed reports keep their entered details and re-enable submission.

Before these fixes, the new regression reproduced eight failures per route. The final `test:listing-action-races` suite passes 26 assertions across both routes and is included in `test:qa`. The 26 editor assertions, save failures, admin/auth failures, session transitions, translations, and 14 PWA/offline checks were rerun successfully.

## Limits

Authenticated saves, messaging, OAuth completion, and payment paths used fixtures. No production listings, messages, accounts, charges, or deployments were created. Native iOS/Android devices, real email/push delivery, and payment settlement were not tested. A save already accepted by the backend cannot be cancelled by navigating away; the guards prevent its stale result from replacing the new form or account UI. This review does not establish that every feature is bug-free.
